The Top 5 Ways to Be a Great Friend

  1. Listen and make eye contact
  2. Be honest, but when being honest approach it in a mature way.
  3. Always be inclusive
  4. Know when to apologize and don't always blame others
  5. Support your friends during good and bad times.

How to Handle Disagreements

Friends are all human and we don't agree on everything. The best thing you can do is listen to what they have to say and not judge them. work through the disagreement calmly. If things get out of hand, give each other some time and apologize when you believe it is right.

Stay calmm listen, and treat others with respect.

Avoid!

  • Talking badly
  • Pressuring someone
  • Ignoring others and only thinking of yourself
  • Trying to everything your firends do
