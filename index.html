Why Friendship Matters

Friends can make life more fun and help us feel supported and everyone deserves a good friend.

Easy Ways to Meet People

  • Smile and become easily approachable
  • say Hi to anyone
  • Join a club at school
  • Invite someone to sit with you
  • Be yourself and give friendships time the dont come easy.

Reminder!!

You do not need to change who you are to make friends. The best friendships are built on honesty.
